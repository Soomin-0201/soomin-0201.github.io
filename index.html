Sloshing and Boil-off of Cryogenic Fuel

  • ⚠️ Challenges
    • Sloshing occurs in many partially-filled fuel tanks, such as launch vehicles.
    • Sloshing triggers dynamic forces, stresses tank structures, and complicates the thermodynamic inside the tank.
    • Cryogenic fuels with extremely low storage temperatures require stricter analysis of sloshing's effects on thermodynamics.
  • 🎯 Goal
      Analyze the effects of sloshing on cryogenic fuel boil-off.
  • 🔬 Approach
      CFD modeling of slosing and boil-off of cryogenic fuel inside the cryo-tank - Volume of Fluid approach.

Full-Mission Aircraft Analysis of Cryogenic Fuel Boil-off

    ⚠️ Challenges
    • For aircraft using cryogenic fuel, boil-off and pressure fluctuations pose design challenges.
    • The evolution of temperature and pressure inside the cryogenic fuel tank throughout a full flight mission is poorly understood.
    🎯 Goal
    • Estimate the temperature and pressure of cryo-tank and boil-off generation during the aircraft mission.
    🔬 Approach
    • Developed a lumped model of a cryogenic fuel tank coupled with a flight mission profile.

Aluminum Propellant Combustion — Nozzle CFD

  • ⚠️ Challenges
    • Aluminum propellant is an energetic material as solid propellants for solid rocket motors.
    • But due to its too high boiling point, aluminum combustion can be incomplete.
    • This reduces the rocket performance.
  • 🎯 Goal
      Understand aluminum combustion physics and predict burning time of aluminum droplets.
  • 🔬 Approach
    • CFD modeling of the full rocket engine nozzle with dispersed burning aluminum particles - Lagrangian-Eulerian approach.
    • CFD modeling of a single droplet under combustion conditions - Volume of Fluid approach.
